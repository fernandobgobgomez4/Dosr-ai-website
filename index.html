Questions

Before you download.

Is DOSR.ai free?

Yes. Logging doses, building stacks, the injection site map, the reconstitution calculator and data export are all free and stay free. Pro removes the limits on AI coach usage and bloodwork scanning, and it comes with a one-week free trial.

Does the app give medical or dosing advice?

No, and it is built specifically not to. DOSR.ai records what you decide to do — it does not tell you what to take, how much, or when. The AI coach answers questions about your own logged data in an educational context. It is not a doctor, it is not a substitute for one, and it will decline to act like one.

What happens to my data?

It's yours. Everything you log is tied to your account, exportable to CSV or JSON whenever you want, and deleted along with your account whenever you choose to delete it. Bloodwork scanning and the AI coach are opt-in, and neither runs unless you turn it on.

Does it work on Android?

Yes. DOSR.ai is on both the App Store and Google Play, with the same feature set on each.

What can I actually track?

Compounds and stacks with doses, units, frequency and cycles; injection sites with rotation history across IM and SubQ locations; bloodwork draws over time; body weight; lifts; and nutrition with hydration. The coach can read across all of it.

Who is it for?

Adults running their own protocols who currently track in a notes app, a spreadsheet, or memory. If you already log your lifts and your macros carefully, this is for the part of your routine that doesn't have a home yet. 18+.

DOSR.ai is a tracking and organization tool, not a medical device or a medical service. It does not diagnose, treat, or prescribe, and nothing in the app or on this site is medical advice. The calculator on this page performs arithmetic on numbers you enter; it does not suggest them. Decisions about any substance you take are between you and a qualified healthcare professional.
